# Artwork brief - "Mango, Jungle Sunrise Gamer" (mango-pfp v1)

Skill: `2d-vector-salik` (Universal 2D Artwork Studio v3.0). Route: Illustrator-first vector, Photoshop light pass (hybrid).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Subject / identity to preserve | Mango: a mango-fruit mascot. Green-yellow top fading to orange and red-orange at the bottom, leaves and a stem on top with water droplets, big dark glossy eyes with two highlights, short green brow marks, rosy blush, small smile, little peach-pink hands and feet (from `ref/mango-reference.png`) |
| Use and size | Discord PFP, square (user's choice); 2000x2000 master, 1080 webp, 512 png; RGB 72 ppi |
| Style | Clean cel-shaded vector game-mascot style: smooth Bezier shapes, 2-3 tone cel shading, warm rim light, crisp illustrated background (never blurred) |
| Pose | Standing on a small mossy log, feet apart, slight lean into the wind; both hands on a gamepad held at the belly; confident smirk with one brow raised; light green cape tied under the chin, billowing left |
| Background | Jungle at sunrise: tall framing trees left and right, hanging vines and swags, layered canopy depth, sun low behind Mango with rays, mist bands, foreground leaves |
| Palette | Orange theme: #ff8a1f orange, #ffb43c amber, #ffe39a sun, #f0603a mango red, #b6e36b cape green, #2f7a3a leaf green, #1f3b2e deep jungle, #3b1d5c dawn violet |
| Light plan | Sun behind Mango, low (back light): warm gold rim on every edge; soft warm fill from the upper left onto the face; cool teal-green shadows in the jungle; face and controller LED are the focal points |
| Text | None |
| Deliverables | .ai, .svg, .psd (one layer per part), master PNG, 2000 jpg, 1080 webp, parts/, qc/ |
| Acceptance | 18-point audit with no open P0/P1; face is the brightest, sharpest area; reads in a 64 px circle; no AI generation, no blurred background |

Reference note: the brief names a second style reference ("Screenshot 2026-10-31 204546.jpg", a Discord banner). It was not attached (only the Mango image arrived, twice) and was not found on disk, so the style follows the skill's clean cel-shaded vector standard instead.
